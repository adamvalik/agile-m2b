# Definition of Done

Initial version, agreed at Sprint 1 Planning (11 November 2026). We review it at every retrospective and record changes at the bottom.

A user story counts as **Done** only when every point below holds. A story that misses one point is not Done and its points are not counted for the sprint.

## Every story

1. All acceptance criteria in the issue are met and ticked.
2. A team member who did not do the work has checked the acceptance criteria.
3. All task sub-issues are closed, and the story is moved to Done on the board.
4. The story is accepted at the Sprint Review.

## Stories with code

5. The change is merged to `main` through a pull request reviewed and approved by at least one other team member.
6. Automated checks pass (build, tests, and the step content check once #4 is in place).
7. The change is live on the public URL and works at phone width and desktop width.
8. The app asks for no account and no personal data beyond EU or non-EU status and arrival date; any saved progress stays on the device.
9. The README is updated if setup, deployment or editing steps changed.

## Stories with content or research

10. Every official step names its official source and a last-checked date.
11. A second team member has reviewed the facts against the sources.
12. Anything we could not confirm is marked as unknown, not stated as fact.
13. Research notes contain no names or identifying details of interviewees.
14. The output is committed in the repo (for example `research/step-facts/`, `design/`, `content/`).
