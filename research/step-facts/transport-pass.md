## Summary for the guide

- **Under 26:** the **südtirolmobil U26 Pass** costs €153 for 365 days or €21 for 30 days. It must be ordered at least one day before the 26th birthday. [1][5][7]
- **26 or older:** the **Fix365 Pass** costs €250 per year, the **Flex Pass** is paid per kilometre (up to €22 per day and €650 per year), and students under 28 can get the **Euregio Ticket Students** for €377 per year. [6][8][10]
- The U26, Fix365 and Flex passes need a valid **Italian tax code**, so the tax code comes first. [1][6][8] The Euregio Ticket Students page doesn't mention one. [10]
- Ordering also needs an **ID card or passport** (team experience; stated on the Flex Pass page). [6]
- The pass is ordered **online** in the südtirolmobil portal. The card is then **picked up in person at an Infopoint**, usually a few days later (team experience). [1][3]
- Until the card is ready, students can buy **single tickets in the südtirolmobil app** with a name and a credit card; no tax code is mentioned. [4]
- Whether **non-EU students** who are not resident in the province can get the U26 Pass is **unknown**. The Flex Pass is explicitly open to people who study in South Tyrol. [5][6]

## Name

- English page: **südtirolmobil U26 Pass**. Italian page: **altoadigemobilità U26 Pass**. German page: **südtirolmobil U26 Pass**. [1][5][7]
- The old **Südtirol Pass abo+** was split into two passes on 1 June 2025, by provincial government resolution No. 356 of 27 May 2025: [13]
  - abo+ for pupils became the **U19 Pass**, for everyone under 19
  - abo+ for students became the **U26 Pass**, for everyone under 26
  
  This is the renaming mentioned in the Product Vision.
- The unibz Study Guide still mentions abo+ in one note about upgrading to the Euregio ticket. That note is out of date. [2]
- The unibz Exchange Students' Guide still describes the old abo+ at €150, ordered online with a declaration that you attend university. That chapter is out of date too. [14]

## Eligibility for the U26 Pass

- **Age: under 26.** All three language versions say the same:
  - EN: "for everyone under 26" and "order the pass at least one day before your 26th birthday" [1]
  - IT: "per tutti sotto i 26 anni" and "almeno un giorno prima del tuo 26° compleanno" [5]
  - DE: "für alle unter 26" and "spätestens einen Tag vor deinem 26. Geburtstag" [7]
  
  So a student who has already turned 26 cannot order it. unibz says the same: under 26 when requesting the pass. [2]
- The ticket finder has a filter "a student ≤ 27 years". [12] The 27 matches the Euregio Ticket Students, which is for students "who have not yet reached the age of 28". [10] It does not extend the U26 Pass, whose own page still says under 26.
- **Residence:** the holder must live in an EU country or Switzerland, or be resident in the Province of Bolzano. [1] The Italian and German pages say "residenza abituale" and "gewöhnlicher Aufenthalt" (habitual residence) in the province. [5][7]
- **Tax code:** a valid Italian tax code is required. [1][5]
- **Being a student is not required:** the passes page says the under-26 offer "does not require you to be enrolled at school or university". [9]
- The pass must be carried together with an ID card or passport when travelling. [1]

## Prices

All prices are from the current pass pages, not from news announcements.

### Under 26

| Pass | Price | Validity | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| U26 Fix365 | €153 | 365 days from the date of issue | [1][5][7] |
| U26 Fix30 | €21 | 30 days from the date of issue, activated online | [1][5][7] |

- Valid until 23:59 on the last day. [1]
- If not renewed, the pass switches automatically to the Flex tariff (pay per kilometre) at no extra cost. [1]
- Covers all public transport in South Tyrol, including regional trains to Trento. Long-distance and high-speed trains can't be used. [1][7]
- Cross-border trains (Brenner–Innsbruck, Innichen–Lienz), Nightliner buses, bikes and pets need an extra ticket. The English page says the pass is "not valid" there; the German page says they cost extra and are not included in the annual price. [1][7]

### 26 or older

| Pass | Price | Who can get it | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fix365 Pass | €250 per year; monthly option from €39 | EU or Swiss residents, or people who work in South Tyrol, with an Italian tax code | [8] |
| Fix30 Pass | €39 for 30 calendar days from the day it is issued; online only | People with a valid Italian tax code; no residence, work or age rule stated | [15] |
| Flex Pass | Pay per km, up to €22 per day and €650 per year | EU or Swiss residents, or people who **work or study** in South Tyrol, with an Italian tax code | [6] |
| Euregio Ticket Students | €377 per year (since 1 April 2026) | Under 28 when applying, enrolled at a university in the Euregio | [10] |

**Flex Pass rates per kilometre in a year** [6]

| Kilometres | Price per km |
|---|---|
| 1–1,000 | 13 cents |
| 1,001–2,000 | 8 cents |
| 2,001–10,000 | 3 cents |
| 10,001–20,000 | 2 cents |
| from 20,001 | free |

The monthly option in the Fix365 row is the Fix30 Pass, a pass of its own with its own page. Unlike the Fix365, it asks only for a tax code, so it matters for non-EU students. If it is not renewed, it switches to the Flex tariff at no extra cost. [15]

unibz recommends the Flex Pass to students over 26 if their annual transport costs do not exceed €250. [2]

The Euregio Ticket Students covers public transport in South Tyrol, Tyrol and Trentino; in Trentino only the services listed on its page. [10] The U26 Pass covers South Tyrol and the regional trains to Trento only. [1]

## How to order

- **Online**, in the südtirolmobil portal (myportal.suedtirolmobil.info). New users register with an email address and personal details. [1][3][9]
- **Payment:** SEPA direct debit, Visa or Mastercard, or pagoPA (fees may apply). An Italian bank account is not needed. [1]
- **Card pick-up:** the card is picked up in person at a südtirolmobil Infopoint. It usually takes only a few days. *Source: team experience; the U26 page does not say how the card is issued.* [1] The Euregio Ticket Students page confirms that its cards are picked up at a südtirolmobil ticket office of the student's choice. [10]
- Ticket offices and authorised points of sale are listed for duplicates and reprints, not for new passes. [1][11] The ticket offices page also says some service and info points offer services for the südtirolmobil Pass and annual passes, but the services it lists for each point are top-ups and duplicates. Whether a new pass can be ordered at an Infopoint is **unknown** (open question 5). [11]

## What you need

- A valid Italian tax code. [1][5]
- An ID card or passport, needed when ordering. *Source: team experience for the U26 Pass.* The Flex Pass page asks for "a digital copy or photo of your ID or passport". [6] The U26 page and the government resolution do not mention it. [1][13]
- Personal details and an email address for the portal account. [1]
- A payment method: SEPA direct debit, Visa or Mastercard, or pagoPA. [1]

## While waiting for the card

- Single tickets can be bought in the südtirolmobil app. The app asks for the ticket holder's name, the number of tickets and credit card details; no tax code is mentioned. [4]
- Tickets can be bought up to 2 months in advance. [4]

## Answers for #39

**Can a non-EU student who is not yet resident in the province get the U26 Pass?**
**Unknown.** The rule allows people who live in an EU country or Switzerland, or who are resident in the Province of Bolzano. [1] It doesn't say whether a non-EU student temporarily living in Bolzano without registered residence counts. The Italian and German pages speak of habitual residence ("residenza abituale", "gewöhnlicher Aufenthalt"), which might cover a student living in Bolzano for a semester. This is our reading; only the Service Desk can confirm it. [5][7]
Alternatives that look open to them:
- the **Flex Pass**, which explicitly includes people who study in South Tyrol [6]
- the **Euregio Ticket Students**, whose page sets no residence rule [10]

Who to ask: südtirolmobil Service Desk, +39 0471 220 880. [3]

**Is the pass ordered online only, or also in person?**
**Online**, through the portal, according to the U26 and passes pages. [1][3][9] The ticket offices page says some info points also offer services for annual passes, but lists only top-ups and duplicates, so ordering in person is not confirmed. [11] The card is then picked up in person at an Infopoint (team experience), which probably explains why a student described the process as in person.

## Open questions

1. Can non-EU students without registered residence in the province get the U26 Pass? (Ask the Service Desk.)
2. Can exchange students get the Euregio Ticket Students, and does it need a tax code? Its page mentions neither. [10]
3. Card pick-up is from team experience only (both Bolzano Infopoints, a few days). Can the Service Desk confirm whether the Bressanone and Brunico Infopoints also hand out new cards?
4. Does a non-EU student need their residence permit or its receipt to order any pass?
5. Can a new pass be ordered at an Infopoint, or only online? The ticket offices page says some info points offer services for annual passes, but lists only top-ups and duplicates. [11] (Ask the Service Desk.)
