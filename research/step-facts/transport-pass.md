## Summary for the guide

- **Under 26:** the **südtirolmobil U26 Pass** costs €153 for 365 days or €21 for 30 days. It must be ordered at least one day before the 26th birthday. [1][5][7]
- **26 or older:** the **Fix365 Pass** costs €250 per year, the **Flex Pass** is paid per kilometre (up to €22 per day and €650 per year), and students under 28 can get the **Euregio Ticket Students** for €377 per year. [6][8][10]
- All these passes need a valid **Italian tax code**, so the tax code comes first. [1][6][8]
- Ordering also needs an **ID card or passport** (team experience; stated on the Flex Pass page). [6]
- The pass is ordered **online** in the südtirolmobil portal. The card is then **picked up in person at an Infopoint**, usually a few days later (team experience). [1][3]
- Until the card is ready, students can buy **single tickets in the südtirolmobil app** with just a name and a credit card. [4]
- Whether **non-EU students** who are not resident in the province can get the U26 Pass is **unknown**. The Flex Pass is explicitly open to people who study in South Tyrol. [5][6]

## Name

- English page: **südtirolmobil U26 Pass**. Italian page: **altoadigemobilità U26 Pass**. German page: **südtirolmobil U26 Pass**. [1][5][7]
- The old **Südtirol Pass abo+** was split into two passes on 1 June 2025, by provincial government resolution No. 356 of 27 May 2025: [13]
  - abo+ for pupils became the **U19 Pass**, for everyone under 19
  - abo+ for students became the **U26 Pass**, for everyone under 26
  
  This is the renaming mentioned in the Product Vision.
- The unibz guide still mentions abo+ in one note about upgrading to the Euregio ticket. That note is out of date. [2]

## Eligibility for the U26 Pass

- **Age: under 26.** All three language versions say the same:
  - EN: "for everyone under 26" and "order the pass at least one day before your 26th birthday" [1]
  - IT: "per tutti sotto i 26 anni" and "almeno un giorno prima del tuo 26° compleanno" [5]
  - DE: "für alle unter 26" and "spätestens einen Tag vor deinem 26. Geburtstag" [7]
  
  So a student who has already turned 26 cannot order it. unibz says the same: under 26 when requesting the pass. [2]
- The ticket finder has a filter "a student ≤ 27 years". [12] It groups all student tickets, and shows U19, U26, Fix30 U26 and Euregio Ticket Students. The 27 matches the Euregio Ticket Students, which is for students "who have not yet reached the age of 28". [10] It does not extend the U26 Pass, whose own page still says under 26.
- **Residence:** the holder must live in an EU country or Switzerland, or be resident in the Province of Bolzano. [1][5]
- **Tax code:** a valid Italian tax code is required. [1][5]
- **Being a student is not required;** enrolment at a school or university is not needed. [9]
- The pass must be carried together with an ID card or passport when travelling. [1]

## Prices

All prices are from the current pass pages, not from news announcements.

### Under 26

| Pass | Price | Validity | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| U26 Fix365 | €153 | 365 days from the date of issue | [1][5][7] |
| U26 Fix30 | €21 | 30 days from the date of issue, activated online | [1][5][7] |

- Valid until 23:59 on the last day. [1]
- If not renewed, the pass switches automatically to the Flex tariff (pay per kilometre) at no extra cost. [1]
- Covers all public transport in South Tyrol, including regional trains to Trento. Not valid on cross-border lines (Brenner–Innsbruck, Vierschach–Lienz), Nightliner buses, long-distance or high-speed trains, or bikes and pets. [1]

### 26 or older

| Pass | Price | Who can get it | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fix365 Pass | €250 per year; monthly option from €39 | EU or Swiss residents, or people who work in South Tyrol, with an Italian tax code | [8] |
| Flex Pass | Pay per km, up to €22 per day and €650 per year | EU or Swiss residents, or people who **work or study** in South Tyrol, with an Italian tax code | [6] |
| Euregio Ticket Students | €377 per year (since 1 April 2026) | Under 28 when applying, enrolled at a university in the Euregio | [10] |

**Flex Pass rates per kilometre in a year** [6]

| Kilometres | Price per km |
|---|---|
| 1–1,000 | 13 cents |
| 1,001–2,000 | 8 cents |
| 2,001–10,000 | 3 cents |
| 10,001–20,000 | 2 cents |
| from 20,001 | free |

unibz recommends the Flex Pass if a student expects to spend less than €250 a year on transport. [2]

The Euregio Ticket Students covers public transport in all of South Tyrol, Tyrol and Trentino. [10] The U26 Pass covers South Tyrol and the regional trains to Trento only. [1]

## How to order

- **Online only**, in the südtirolmobil portal (myportal.suedtirolmobil.info). New users register with an email address and personal details. [1][3]
- **Payment:** SEPA direct debit, Visa or Mastercard, or pagoPA (fees may apply). An Italian bank account is not needed. [1]
- **Card pick-up:** the card is picked up in person at a südtirolmobil Infopoint. It usually takes only a few days. *Source: team experience; the U26 page does not say how the card is issued.* [1] The Euregio Ticket Students page confirms that its cards are picked up at a südtirolmobil ticket office of the student's choice. [10]
- Ticket offices and authorised points of sale are listed for duplicates and reprints, not for new passes. [1][11]

## What you need

- A valid Italian tax code. [1][5]
- An ID card or passport, needed when ordering. *Source: team experience for the U26 Pass.* The Flex Pass page asks for "a digital copy or photo of your ID or passport". [6] The U26 page and the government resolution do not mention it. [1][13]
- Personal details and an email address for the portal account. [1]
- A payment method: SEPA direct debit, Visa or Mastercard, or pagoPA. [1]

## While waiting for the card

- Single tickets can be bought in the südtirolmobil app. Only the ticket holder's name and a credit card are needed; no tax code is mentioned. [4]
- Tickets can be bought up to 2 months in advance. [4]

## Answers for #39

**Can a non-EU student who is not yet resident in the province get the U26 Pass?**
**Unknown.** The rule allows people who live in an EU country or Switzerland, or who are resident in the Province of Bolzano. [1][5] It doesn't say whether a non-EU student temporarily living in Bolzano without registered residence counts.
Alternatives that look open to them:
- the **Flex Pass**, which explicitly includes people who study in South Tyrol [6]
- the **Euregio Ticket Students**, whose page sets no residence rule [10]

Who to ask: südtirolmobil Service Desk, +39 0471 220 880. [3]

**Is the pass ordered online only, or also in person?**
**Online only**, through the portal. [1][3] The card is then picked up in person at an Infopoint (team experience), which probably explains why a student described the process as in person.
