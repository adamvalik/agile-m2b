**Codes added in this pass** (not in the guide's list):

| Code | Theme | Why added |
| --- | --- | --- |

| SAFE | Personal safety in the city | Added 2026-10-29 for W44 |

Second pass on 2026-10-29: Google Maps reviews of offices (W31 to W38), read in Chrome while signed in. Maps shows reviews with relative dates ("a year ago"), so their posting dates are approximate; reviews were read through Google's automatic translation. Staff names that appear in reviews are not recorded.

**Posted** is the date the post was written. For PDF reports it is the file's creation date, since most reports carry no date of their own.
