# Competitor Research: Interactive Guides for Incoming Students

Team **M2B** – research for the unibz incoming-student guide project
Date: 4 November 2026

## Goal

Find existing interactive websites and apps (not static PDFs) that guide exchange students or freshers through their first weeks, so we can understand current solutions, spot gaps and position our project.

## Summary

Existing tools mostly split into two camps:

- **Institutional roadmap tools** (Paris-Saclay, Sorbonne, Erasmus+ App) that handle the administrative order of steps.
- **Social / peer platforms** (Goin', Erasmus Play) that handle community and shared knowledge.

None of the tools found combines an **adaptive, dependency-aware checklist** (EU or non-EU, arrival date) with **city-specific detail for Bolzano**. That is the gap M2B can target.

## 1. Personalised "what to do, in what order" tools (closest competitors)

| Tool | Type | What it does | Relevance to M2B |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Université Paris-Saclay – Online Welcome Desk** | Web platform | Generates a personalised roadmap of administrative steps before and after arrival in France, based on the student's situation. Built on a regulatory database; other French institutions can adopt and rebrand it. Answers FAQs 24/7. | Closest match to our concept. Worth studying in detail. |
| **Sorbonne University – International Welcome Desk** | Web platform | Single gateway to university services, from arrival preparation to departure, organised by moments: before arrival, during the stay, before departure. | Good model for structuring content by phase. |
| **Erasmus+ App (EU official)** | Mobile + web app | Step-by-step checklists for mobility milestones, push notifications from home and host universities, Learning Agreement status, tips from past participants, ESN events, cost-of-living data per destination. | Overlaps our problem areas 1, 2 and 4, but generic and not Bolzano-specific. |
| **Università di Bologna – IWD app** | Mobile app | Non-EU students register and download their codice fiscale certificate in the app. | Narrow but directly relevant: digitises one painful Italian step. |

## 2. University-built interactive onboarding portals

| Tool | Institution | What it does | Takeaway |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Onboarding international students** | VU Amsterdam (NL) | Guides admitted students through enrolment and practical matters (municipality registration, GP, insurance), plus student ambassadors, a blog and short video clips. | Mixes official steps with peer stories. |
| **Be Prepared** | University of Chichester (UK) | Pre-arrival site with actions and checklists to help students prepare proactively. | Simple checklist-driven approach. |
| **iHawk pre-arrival checklist** | University of Iowa (US) | Logged-in checklist; students click each item and follow instructions to complete it. | Shows the value of tracked progress per student. |
| **NCU OnBoard** | Nicolaus Copernicus University (PL) | Digital guide with step-by-step help on academic and everyday life in Toruń and Bydgoszcz. | City-specific daily-life guidance, like our area 3. |
| **Welcome to OVGU** | OVGU Magdeburg (DE) | App built jointly by a student initiative and the International Office. | Student + office partnership model could suit a unibz pitch. |

## 3. Community and peer-knowledge platforms (our area 4)

| Tool | What it does | Takeaway |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Goin'** | Social community app used by many universities (Oslo, Utrecht, Antwerp, Tampere, Gothenburg and others). One-to-one connections, groups by interest, programme or nationality, an arrival guide and a world map of incoming students. | Strong on community, light on administrative guidance. Commercial, sold to universities. |
| **Erasmus Play** | Claims coverage of 500+ Erasmus destinations and partnerships with 85 universities. Meet students going to the same destination, join destination chats, find accommodation, short video tips. | Peer knowledge per destination, but not tied to official procedures. |

## 4. General "everything for your studies" apps

| Tool | What it does | Takeaway |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Studo** | Popular in Austria, Germany and Slovenia (450,000+ students). Calendar, timetable, university email, news feed and chat in one app. | Focused on daily study life, not arrival. Shows what students in the region already expect from a student app. |

## Implications for M2B

1. **Differentiator:** an adaptive checklist that understands dependencies between steps (codice fiscale → SIM → bank → transport pass → residence permit) and adapts to EU/non-EU status and arrival date.
2. **Local depth:** Bolzano-specific offices, addresses and bilingual context, which generic tools (Erasmus+ App, Erasmus Play) lack.
3. **"Do it from home" focus:** surface steps that can be done before arrival (our area 2); few tools frame content this way.
4. **Later candidates:** a peer-tips layer (like Erasmus+ Top Tips or Goin') and a map of useful places (like NCU OnBoard), matching areas 3 and 4.
5. **Benchmark to study first:** Paris-Saclay Online Welcome Desk, as the closest existing implementation of a personalised roadmap.
